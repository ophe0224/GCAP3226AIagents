Introduction
This essay reflects on my experience drafting a government enquiry for the flu vaccination project in Hong Kong. The assignment required me to assess data governance, draft a professional enquiry, and collaborate with an AI agent to support my research and policy analysis.

Main Body
I learned that while some health data is available, recent and detailed statistics—such as age-specific or district-level vaccination rates—are not shown and require formal requests. Identifying the exact data needed was challenging, as information is fragmented and not always clearly described. Drafting the enquiry letter taught me the importance of clarity, structure, and referencing the Code on Access to Information in formal government communication.

The AI agent was time-saving in organizing my requests, but I needed to provide sufficient background information for it to be effective. Compared to a chatbot tutor, the agent offered more actionable feedback and helped me develop skills in data analysis and professional writing. Access to government data will strengthen our analysis, but if requests are denied, we will rely on published reports and academic studies.

Conclusion
This process deepened my understanding of data-driven policymaking and the practicalities of government data access. I am now better prepared to request data and use AI support for future research.
